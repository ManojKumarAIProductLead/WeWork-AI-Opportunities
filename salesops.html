Workflow — an enterprise expansion request
1
Enterprise admin messages "we need 40 more seats at Prestige from next month" via WorkPal or email.
2
Seat-Flex Agent checks live inventory in the booking system, confirms availability, computes prorated pricing from the contract's rate card.
3
Quote Agent drafts an order-form amendment. If within the rep's discount authority → straight to the admin; if not → approval gate to the account owner.
4
On e-sign, Contract Agent files the amendment to CRM and Billing Agent updates the next invoice automatically.
5
Pipeline Agent logs the expansion as an upsell win and updates account health. Total human effort: one approval click.
The principle: agents do drafting, checking, and reconciling. Humans own every commercial commitment via approval gates. Low integration risk — agents read/write through existing CRM and billing APIs.

Success measures & business case
Measure 1 — velocity
Quote-to-signature cycle time
Target: median enterprise quote turnaround from 2–3 days to under 4 hours; renewals initiated 90 days ahead instead of reactive.
Measure 2 — cost to serve
Sales + finance admin hours per closed seat
Target: cut admin effort per deal ~40%, freeing reps to sell and finance to focus on genuine exceptions, not data entry.
Business case: faster quotes lift win-rate on competitive enterprise deals; proactive renewals protect the long-term leasing base (a core KPI); automated billing recon reduces leakage and DSO. If ~11 hours/rep/week shifts from admin to selling, a 30-person commercial team reclaims the equivalent of 8 full-time sellers — at the cost of one quarter of agent build + integration.
