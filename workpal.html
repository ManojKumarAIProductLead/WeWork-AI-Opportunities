From support bot to one-stop hub cross-sell / up-sell built in
WorkPal isn't just deflecting tickets — every conversation is a context-aware moment to surface a value-added service. Because the agent knows who the member is, where they are, and what they're doing, it can offer the right thing at the right time without feeling like an ad.
Booking a 6-person room → offer catering & AV setup
Hitting day-pass limit 3rd time → suggest All-Access upgrade
Team growing → flag private-office options & intro to sales
Frequent late stays → parking pass & wellness add-ons

Success measures & business case
Measure 1 — deflection
% intents resolved without human escalation
Target: 60%+ of routine member queries fully automated, cutting support cost while improving response time 7×.
Measure 2 — monetization
VAS attach rate via in-chat offers
Target: lift value-added-service attach and average ticket size by converting support moments into contextual upsell.
Business case: WorkPal hits two KPIs at once — it lowers cost-to-serve (est. ₹18L/month at current scale) and raises member LTV by making every interaction a service-and-sell channel at near-zero marginal cost.
