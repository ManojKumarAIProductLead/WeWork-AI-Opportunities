SpaceIQ
Enterprise workspace intelligence dashboard
Live
Enterprise clients managing hundreds of seats across multiple WeWork centers have zero visibility into how their space is actually being used. SpaceIQ gives GCCs and MNCs a real-time analytics layer — seat utilization by center, cost benchmarking against peers, AI-generated capacity recommendations, and demand heat maps — turning WeWork from a landlord into a strategic workspace partner.
  • Center-by-center utilization tracking across Bengaluru, Gurugram, Mumbai, Chennai, Hyderabad
  • AI recommendations panel — flags underutilized centers, capacity risks, cost benchmarks vs peer GCCs
  • Day-of-week heat map to inform anchor-day and hybrid attendance policies
  • Quarter-on-quarter trends: utilization, cost/seat, NPS, service requests
  • Interactive 6-month utilization trend chart across all centers
Business case: Every enterprise client that can see their underutilized seats is more likely to right-size at renewal and expand where genuinely needed. Reduces churn, enables data-driven upsell. Value-added services are ~14–15% of WeWork India's revenue — SpaceIQ is the intelligence layer that makes them defensible.

2
CommunityGraph
AI-powered member matching and connections
Live
WeWork's original promise was community — but today it's mostly just events. CommunityGraph adds an AI matching layer that surfaces the right members to connect with based on role, skills, domain, attendance patterns, and shared interests — turning the physical co-presence of thousands of professionals into a genuine, defensible network effect.
  • AI match scoring with explainable reasoning — not just tags, but narrative "why this person" context
  • Location-aware suggestions using check-in data — surfaces people you're likely to physically encounter
  • Curated event recommendations showing which matches are already attending
  • Member cluster insights — dominant skill groups, collaboration activity, cross-company intros
  • Privacy-first — matching uses behavioral signals, never personal data
Business case: Members who form meaningful connections renew at significantly higher rates. CommunityGraph creates a data asset — a professional graph across WeWork India's 100,000+ desk network — that no competitor can replicate without the same physical footprint.
