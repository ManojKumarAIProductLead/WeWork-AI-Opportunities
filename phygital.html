Phygital — connecting the physical workspace
Two breakthrough-but-buildable bets that turn the physical space into real-time digital signals — one to make the member experience frictionless, one to make operations proactive. Each ships as a single-center MVP first.
SmartPass
NFC + geofenced frictionless access, check-in, and pay
MVP: 1 center, ~8 weeks
The phone becomes the key, the booking, and the wallet. Geofence detects arrival and auto-checks-in the member; NFC taps open turnstiles, meeting rooms, printers, and the café; everything settles to one monthly statement. No plastic cards, no front-desk queue, no "which room was I in?" — and every tap is a usage signal that feeds SpaceIQ and billing.
MVP system design
Member phone
WeWork app · geofence (arrival) + NFC/HCE (tap)
Edge — at the center
NFC readers on doors, rooms, printers, café POS
Access & event service
Validates entitlement, logs event, emits usage signal
Downstream
Auto check-in · consolidated billing · SpaceIQ utilization feed
Why it's feasible: NFC host-card-emulation and geofencing are standard mobile APIs; most WeWork access hardware already speaks to a controller. MVP wraps the existing access system with an event bus — no new turnstiles required.
Measure 1
Front-desk check-in load
Target: 80% of entries self-service via SmartPass; reception time redirected to hospitality, not gatekeeping.
Measure 2
Ancillary spend capture
Target: +15% café/printer/meeting-room revenue captured per active member via one-tap pay vs. manual logging.
Business case: lifts average ticket size (a core KPI) by making ancillary spend frictionless, while the usage exhaust improves occupancy data quality for every other product. Hardware-light, so it scales center-by-center at low capex.

SenseSpace
Sensor-driven, self-optimizing space operations
MVP: 1 floor, ~10 weeks
Low-cost occupancy, air-quality, and energy sensors make the building self-aware. Empty-but-booked rooms auto-release after a grace period; housekeeping is dispatched by actual usage, not a fixed rota; HVAC and lighting follow real occupancy; poor air quality triggers an alert before members complain. Ops shifts from reactive tickets to predictive nudges.
MVP system design
Sensors (edge)
PIR occupancy · CO₂/air quality · energy meters · desk presence
Gateway
LoRaWAN / WiFi gateway aggregates + timestamps readings
Rules + ML engine
Ghost-booking detection · housekeeping demand model · anomaly alerts
Actions
Auto-release rooms · dispatch ops tasks · HVAC/lighting control · SpaceIQ feed
Why it's feasible: battery sensors + LoRaWAN need no rewiring and install in days. Start with rules (release a room with zero occupancy 15 min after start); layer ML once a few weeks of data exists. One floor proves the unit economics before rollout.
Measure 1
Recovered bookable hours
Target: reclaim 15–20% of "booked but empty" room-hours via auto-release — directly lifts effective capacity and revenue per sq ft.
Measure 2
Energy cost per sq ft
Target: 8–12% energy reduction through occupancy-linked HVAC/lighting, without member comfort complaints.
Business case: recovered room-hours and lower energy both improve revenue per sq ft (a core KPI) and margin — the metric Virwani says flows straight to the bottom line. Usage-based housekeeping cuts ops cost while improving the member experience. Sensor payback is typically well under a year per floor.
