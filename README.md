# WeWork India — AI Opportunities

AI product concepts and interactive prototypes exploring how WeWork India can leverage AI to power its Workspace-as-a-Service strategy.

## Prototypes

### SpaceIQ — Enterprise Workspace Intelligence
An analytics dashboard for enterprise clients (GCCs, MNCs) to track seat utilization, cost benchmarking, and AI-driven capacity recommendations across all their WeWork centers.

**Live preview:** Open `spaceiq-dashboard.html` in any browser.

**Key features:**
- Center-by-center utilization tracking across cities (Bengaluru, Gurugram, Mumbai, Chennai, Hyderabad)
- AI recommendations panel — flags underutilized centers, capacity risks, and cost benchmarks vs peers
- Day-of-week heat map for anchor-day policy planning
- Quarter-on-quarter trend tracking (utilization, cost/seat, NPS, service requests)
- Interactive utilization trend chart across all centers

**Strategic fit:** Directly supports WeWork India's Workspace-as-a-Service pivot. Turns WeWork from a landlord into a strategic enterprise partner — reducing churn and enabling data-driven renewal and expansion conversations.
