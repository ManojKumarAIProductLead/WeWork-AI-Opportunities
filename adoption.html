Growing app downloads and DAU/MAU
Most features already exist; downloads sit at ~40% with low daily use. The common thread across the ideas below: tie the app to actions members already take — entry, daily work tasks, services — and add a GenAI layer over systems that already run.

Context: most members are SMB and GCC employees. They book seats for a period, enter with a keycard or NFC, and use offline amenities (café, printers). Each of those has a frictionless non-app path today, so the app stays optional. The three parts below cover near-term mechanics, personalising what exists, and structural bets — flagged where they're GenAI or already built in this portfolio.
Three parts to grow DAU / MAU
1
Onsite & offsite growth mechanics
Acquisition — get the download, get the first action
Offsite
  • CRM campaigns to member-company admins and employees at onboarding and renewal.
  • Admin-led provisioning — office admin sets up access digitally, pulling the whole team onto the app.
  • In-company referral — invite colleagues to book adjacent desks, the same room, or join a team event.
Onsite
  • QR nudges on physical surfaces — desks, meeting rooms, café, printers — each opening one app action via App Clip / Instant App, no full install first, then prompt to keep the app.
  • Day-one setup required to get desk, wifi, and access on the first visit — uses unavoidable first-day friction as the trigger.
  • In-center screens & signage tied to app-only actions: event RSVP, extend a room, order from the café.
2
Improve & personalise existing features
Engagement — make what exists faster, smarter, and worth opening
  • Make daily basics app-first — booking, wifi join, guest passes, and support faster through the app than the offline path.
  • GenAI concierge (WorkPal) — natural-language booking, support, and service requests over existing booking/helpdesk/billing APIs. GenAI built
  • Personalised home screen from behavioural data — usual desk, usual coffee, calendar gaps — built from app and badge activity, not ops-heavy events. GenAI
  • Proactive nudges — "90-minute gap at 2 — book the calm room or a 4pm class?" — from calendar and presence context only WeWork holds. GenAI
  • Useful-only notifications — room in 10 minutes, delivery arrived, invoice ready — not generic broadcasts.
3
Big bets
Structural — make the app part of a daily, often mandatory action
These tie the app to something members do daily or must do, so usage stops being optional. Each is asset-lite and ops-lite — wrapping systems that already run rather than rebuilding them.
Mobile keycard as default entry
covered
Entry is the only 100%-frequency daily action. Make the phone the default key; the physical card becomes a fallback, then is deprecated center by center. Converts entry into a daily app open. Managed migration, not a hard switch-off.
Employer SaaS suite — one-stop employee console
GenAI
An AI-powered offering for member companies covering HRMS-lite (leave, attendance from check-in data), travel booking, benefits & reimbursement, and credits/wellness. Employees do these weekly or monthly anyway — if they live in the WeWork app, the app becomes essential, not optional. Wraps existing HRMS / travel / expense tools via integrations; a conversational LLM layer handles "file this reimbursement", "book my travel", "what's my leave balance".
Talent marketplace — expertise between members
GenAI
A Topmate / Toptal-style layer where members offer and find coaching, mentoring, advisory, and freelance projects across the WeWork network. LLM matches a need to the right expert using the member graph; WeWork takes a take-rate. Gives members a reason to open the app beyond desk booking, and deepens the community moat. Builds on CommunityGraph.
Stored-value wallet + café / amenity order-ahead
Order coffee or lunch from the desk, skip the queue; preload credits. The order-ahead utility is a daily reason to open the app; stored preferences and balance raise switching cost.
VAS marketplace in WorkPal
built
Cult.fit, Klay, Swiggy, IT supplies, wellness, meditation — booked and billed in-app, asset-lite. Already prototyped in the WorkPal demo.
The GenAI layer — LLMs on top of existing systems
  • Conversational concierge over booking, support, and billing APIs — WorkPal.
  • Natural-language employer console over HRMS, travel, and expense tools — the SaaS suite.
  • Need-to-expert matching over the member graph — the talent marketplace.
  • Personalised nudges from behavioural, calendar, and presence data — the home screen and proactive prompts.
Each sits on systems WeWork already runs, so the build is an integration-and-orchestration effort, not a rebuild.
Success measures
Measure 1
Daily app-open rate among present members
Of members who badge in on a given day, the share who opened the app that day — the clearest signal that the app is on the daily path.
Measure 2
Day-1 setup completion for new members
New joiners completing app setup on their first visit — addresses the members who never download at the source.
Business case: the monetisation bets in this portfolio — VAS marketplace, employer suite, talent marketplace, stored-value — all depend on a daily-active base. Growing DAU/MAU is the gate that unlocks the rest.
